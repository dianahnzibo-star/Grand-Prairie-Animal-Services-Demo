About Us

Welcome to Grand Prairie Animal Services

We are dedicated to providing a safe and caring environment for animals while helping them connect with loving families.

This site makes it easier for visitors to explore adoptable pets, submit an inquiry, learn about adoption status, and get involved as a volunteer.

Whether you are adopting, volunteering, or simply learning more, your support helps animals move one step closer to a forever home.

Helping pets find safe, loving forever homes.
